## Reusable process lessons

- Maintain a source-document register.
- Preserve original inputs and generated outputs separately.
- Record exact report test criteria.
- Check critical text at expected locations.
- Keep failed checks visible.
- Keep outputs marked DRAFT until engineering review.
- Maintain a living charter with decisions, risks, limitations, and evidence.

## Future reuse requirement

Before any causeway asset is reused in the slab product, document the target
component, applicable slab standard, source revision, unit mapping, expected
values, and independent engineering approval.
