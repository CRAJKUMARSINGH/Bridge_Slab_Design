# DEV-007 — Afflux tolerance 0.08 m absolute with no engineering approval

- Opened: 2026-10-26
- Status: RULED (owner W06, closing week W06) — registry approved; the code-side change is tracked separately below and is still open
- Source of truth: creat.md Week 6 — rtol=1e-6 unless engineering review approves a different tolerance
- Observed: engineConsistency.test.ts used a bare 0.08 m absolute tolerance with no approval record

## Evidence
- Previous code: `expect(Math.abs(excel.afflux - web.afflux)).toBeLessThanOrEqual(0.08)`
- At a typical afflux of 0.2 m, this allows ~40% relative drift
- Afflux directly drives the freeboard requirement and the deck level
- foundation/tolerances.json now records rtol=0.05 / atol=0.02 with an approval (the "PENDING" placeholder is gone)

## IRC Reference
IRC:SP:13-2004 / IRC:SP:82-2015 freeboard requirements depend on accurate afflux calculation.
A 40% error in afflux could shift freeboard by the full design margin.

## Engineer ruling — 2026-10-26
Approver: RAJKUMAR SINGH CHAUHAN, Fellow IEI, reg. no. 1293016.

Ruling: the afflux tolerance is accepted at rtol=0.05 / atol=0.02 (Molesworth). The widening was
**explicitly reviewed and accepted** — it was not a verbal or assumed approval. The same ruling
accepts the eight quantity entries held at atol 1e-6, each of which is 1000x wider than the
charter's 1e-9 default atol and had carried no recorded approver.

The magnitude is stated here so the ruling is not read as paperwork: afflux at 0.05 rtol is
~50,000x looser than the charter's 1e-6 rtol, and its 0.02 atol is ~20 million times the 1e-9
default. Both numbers are now a licensed ruling, not a guess. Any future change to either
requires a new approver and a new date.

Recorded per entry in foundation/tolerances.json with `approvedBy` + `approvedOn` = 2026-10-26.
