A — Project Identity and Source File Register
THE JAKHAM RIVER — ENGINEERING CONTEXT: The Jakham is a southward-flowing perennial river in the tribal belt of Dungarpur and Pratapgarh districts, joining the Som River near Dhariyawad. Its monsoon hydrology is strongly influenced by the orographic rainfall on the Aravalli–Vindhya watershed; peak discharges can exceed 1,000 cumecs in exceptional years. The Mandvi–Parsola–Bajpura road serves predominantly Adivasi communities whose access to markets, hospitals, and schools is cut during the monsoon months whenever the river is in flood. A submersible bridge at this location — rather than a ford — provides a low-cost, durable all-year crossing that is trafficable for 8–10 months per year, which is sufficient to meet the community's connectivity needs under the PMGSY and NABARD rural infrastructure mandate.
PROJECT SCOPE: This report covers the submersible bridge across the Jakham River on the Mandvi–Parsola via Bajpura road. The Jakham is a seasonal river in southern Rajasthan with substantial monsoon discharges. The project has an unusually complete documentary trail — design notes, site visit bills, quantity-surveying bills, a design bill, and a forwarding letter — all preserved as DOC files in Attached_Assets (Git LFS).

B — Hydraulic Analysis and BEDACH Stability Sequence
HYDRAULIC ANALYSIS (IRC SP-13): The Jakham River at the Mandvi–Parsola–Bajpura crossing is characterised by high monsoon discharge with a moderate bed slope typical of the Aravalli piedmont. Manning's equation applied to the cross-section establishes the discharge, velocity, and Froude number. Lacey regime width governs the waterway. The Molesworth afflux and IRC:78-1983 scour depth are computed from the same hydraulic parameters. The submersible design accepts overtopping; buoyancy and current forces govern.
BEDACH LOAD SEQUENCE: (A) Dead Load — superstructure + substructure with 100% buoyancy at design flood. (B) Live Load — IRC Class-A or 70R maximum pier reaction from LLCALpier sheet. (C) Water Current — longitudinal and transverse components at 20° per IRC:6 Cl. 213.5. (D) Seismic — Zone II, not governing; declared per IRC:6 Cl. 222.1. (E) Wind — per IRC:6 Cl. 212.3 by height zone. Base Pressure compared against SBC: p_max ≤ SBC; p_min ≥ 0.

C — Abutment Design and Project Documentation Trail
ABUTMENT (IRC:78-1983) — TYPE 1 GRAVITY (INFERRED): The Jakham project does not have a separately named Abutment Design XLS. The abutment stability check (sliding, overturning, bearing) is embedded within the Stability Analysis XLS. The abutment is a gravity (Type 1) mass concrete structure consistent with the submersible bridge height range (H ≤ 5 m). Active earth pressure is per Rankine Ka = (1−sinφ)/(1+sinφ); live-load surcharge hs = 1.2 m. All three failure mode checks must be confirmed in the Stability XLS by the checking engineer. A dedicated Abutment XLS and TYPE1-GRAVITY-ABUTMENT.dxf drawing must be added to Attached_Assets before construction drawings are issued.
