A — Project Identity and Construction Stage Context
HYDRAULIC IDENTITY OF THE SOM AT LARATHI: The Som River at Larathi drains approximately 4,450 km² of the southern Aravalli piedmont, making it one of the largest tributaries of the Mahi system in Rajasthan. At the Larathi crossing, the design discharge is Q = 1,066.8 cumecs (50-year return period), the recorded Highest Flood Level is HFL = 99.50 m MSL, and the mean bed level sits at 96.17 m MSL, giving a total flow depth of 3.33 m at design flood. The adopted waterway — 12 spans of 8.0 m clear — provides a total waterway of 96 m, consistent with the Lacey regime width for this discharge magnitude and bed material (silt factor f ≈ 1.0 for sandy alluvium). The foundation is taken to 92.0 m MSL, 4.17 m below the mean bed level, to satisfy the 1.33× design scour depth criterion of IRC:78-1983. These parameters are traceable to the "Stability Analysis SUBMERSIBLE BRIDGE ACROSS LARATHI SOM RIVER.xls" workbook in Git LFS.
PROJECT SCOPE: This document covers the Som River submersible bridge on the Larathi–Larathi B road, dated 28.06.2022. The Som River is a perennial stream in the Udaipur district of Rajasthan. B — Hydraulic and Structural Design Narrative
HYDRAULIC ANALYSIS (IRC SP-13): The Som River is a perennial stream with moderate monsoon peak flows in the Aravalli foothills region. The design discharge is established from the project hydrological study. Manning's equation applied to the surveyed cross-section gives the velocity and Froude number. Lacey regime width governs the waterway provision. Molesworth afflux and IRC:78-1983 design scour depth establish the minimum foundation depth. As a submersible bridge, overtopping at design flood is accepted; buoyancy and current forces are the primary structural destabilisers.
STRUCTURAL DESIGN — BEDACH SEQUENCE (IRC:6-2016): (A) Dead Load: slab, wearing coat, pier cap, pier shaft, footing — with 100% buoyancy at design flood. (B) Live Load: IRC Class-A or 70R governing vehicle arrangement; impact factor included. (C) Water Current and Buoyancy: longitudinal and transverse current forces per IRC:6-2016 Cl. 213.5 at 20° skew; 100% buoyancy at footing level, 15% effective at pier shaft per IRC:78-1983. (D) Seismic: per IRC:6-2016 Cl. 219 — zone-appropriate horizontal coefficient applied; Zone II or III for Rajasthan (check source XLS for zone). (E) Wind: per IRC:6-2016 Cl. 209. Base pressure: p_max ≤ SBC; p_min ≥ 0.
