A — Complete Design File Register
THE FORCE PATH — FROM DECK TO SOIL: Every bridge design tells the story of how loads travel from the surface where vehicles run down to the soil that ultimately bears them. At the Gumaniya Wala Nalah bridge, that force path is exceptionally well-documented. A loaded truck crosses the deck slab (IRC Class-A or 70R); the slab bends, transferring its reactions to the pier cap at two bearing points. The pier cap — the critical transverse beam sitting atop the pier shaft — must resist these concentrated reactions in bending and shear without cracking or crushing. The pier shaft then carries the combined vertical load plus moments (from eccentricity, water current, wind, and seismic) downward to the footing. The footing distributes these forces to the founding soil over its plan area, and the soil's safe bearing capacity (SBC) determines whether the bridge stands or sinks. This collection has three pier cap design workbooks for the Gumaniya project — the project-specific "GUMANIYA WALA NALAH" XLS, the "sheladia example" XLS, and the general "Design of Pier Cap" XLS — which together provide both the project calculation and a peer-reviewed reference example, enabling the checking engineer to verify the pier cap design independently.
PROJECT IDENTITY: The Gumaniya Wala Nalah submersible bridge near the Aaloo (potato) Factory in Udaipur is notable for having the most complete pier cap design record in the collection. Two pier cap design XLS files are available: the "GUMANIYA WALA NALAH NEAR AALOO FACTORY, UDAIPUR" specific one, and the general "Design of Pier Cap sheladia example" variant and general "Design of Pier Cap" workbook — all three stored in Git LFS. The full design chain covers hydraulics → stability → pier cap → abutment, enabling end-to-end verification. The UIT Udaipur authority is the project sponsor.

B — Hydraulic Analysis and BEDACH Pier Stability
HYDRAULIC ANALYSIS (IRC SP-13): The "Hydraulic Calculations Gumania Wala Nalah Bridge Near Aaloo factory.doc" establishes the design discharge, HFL, velocity, Lacey regime width, afflux, and scour depth for the Gumaniya Wala Nalah. The nala is a minor urban drainage channel with flashy monsoon behaviour; the design discharge from the catchment analysis is the primary hydraulic input. The general hydraulics.xls template may have been used as the calculation aid. The submersible bridge accepts overtopping; buoyancy and current forces govern the stability checks.
PIER CAP DESIGN (IRC:112-2015): The pier cap carries the bearing reactions from the superstructure deck slab and distributes them to the pier shaft. The cap is designed as a simply supported deep beam (or corbel depending on geometry) for bending and shear. The "Design of Pier Cap GUMANIYA WALA NALAH NEAR AALOO FACTORY, UDAIPUR.xls" performs this check. The "sheladia example" and general pier cap XLS files provide worked examples for cross-reference. The cap dimensions must be consistent with the pier body dimensions used in the stability analysis — this cross-file consistency must be confirmed by the checking engineer.
