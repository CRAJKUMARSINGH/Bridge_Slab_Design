A — Design Basis: 75 Tonne Heavy Loading
WHY 75 TONNES — THE ENGINEERING IMPERATIVE: The Parwan River lies on a mineral transit corridor in Rajasthan: the hills flanking the Parwan valley contain significant deposits of limestone, marble, and silica sand that are transported by heavy multi-axle trucks throughout the year. The 75-tonne design loading is the IRC classification equivalent to a Class 70R tracked vehicle (70 tonnes) or a two-axle special transport with appropriate dynamic amplification. In the 2006 IRC:6 regime under which this bridge was designed, 75T loading governed pier design through a substantially higher braking force, a larger eccentricity moment, and a correspondingly greater rotational destabilisation at the pier base than the standard Class-A loading. The SBC of 75 t/m² = 750 kN/m² — documented in the design file — points to founding on hard Precambrian quartzite or granite of the Aravalli basement rock, which confirms the site geology and justifies the open foundation approach. No pile or well foundation is needed when bearing on competent hard rock, but the design must verify that the rock surface is truly sound and unfractured across the full footing plan area.
HEAVY LOADING CONTEXT: The Parwan River bridge is designed for 75 tonne loading — the heaviest vehicle class in the old IRC:6 regime. This corresponds approximately to the IRC Class 70R tracked vehicle or a special-transport vehicle. The SBC cited in the design file is 75 t/m² = 750 kN/m², which is extremely high and suggests founding on hard rock — consistent with the rocky terrain of the Aravalli region in Rajasthan where the Parwan flows. The design date 190906 (19 September 2006) places it in the pre-IRC:6-2016 era, so the governing load factors are those of the earlier IRC:6-2000 or IRC:6-1966 revision. The checking engineer must confirm which revision was used and whether the load factors need updating to the 2016 edition for submission to current authorities.

B — Structural Stability — BEDACH Sequence (Heavy Loading)
BEDACH SEQUENCE WITH 75T LOADING: The heavy vehicle load of 75 tonnes significantly increases the live load pier reaction compared to a standard Class-A vehicle. The braking force (longitudinal load) is proportionally larger, and the transverse eccentricity moment is also amplified. The stability analysis must address these increased moments in all load combinations. The abutment DOC "ABUTMENT PARWAN.doc" provides the abutment calculation narrative in Word format; the XLS provides the numerical workbook. The design report DOC "design parwan bridge 1900906 SBC 75 TONNE.doc" provides the complete narrative design report for submission.

C — Abutment Design (Type 2 — Cantilever RCC Likely, IRC:78-1983 + IRC:112-2015)
ABUTMENT (IRC:78-1983 + IRC:112-2015) — TYPE 2 CANTILEVER (LIKELY): The Parwan River 75-Tonne heavy-load bridge is a high-level structure with significant live loading. The "Abutment PARWAN DOC" (Git LFS) contains the abutment design narrative and calculations. Given the 75T loading (IRC Class 70R + special heavy vehicles), the horizontal braking force transmitted to the abutment cap is substantially higher than standard Class-A designs, and the approach embankment height for a Parwan River crossing is likely to exceed 4.5 m — making a Type 2 cantilever RCC abutment the appropriate choice. The abutment DOC should be reviewed to confirm the adopted type, dimensions, and material. A dedicated Abutment XLS (converting the DOC calculations into a tabulated format) and a TYPE2-CANTILEVER-ABUTMENT.dxf drawing must be prepared and added to Attached_Assets before the project is submitted for PWD/NHAI approval.
