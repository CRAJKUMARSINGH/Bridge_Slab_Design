IRC Bridge Design — Quick Reference

Key formulas, standard clauses, and load-combination tables drawn from the IRC suite applicable to the 16 bridge projects in this collection. All values and clause numbers are cross-referenced to the editions used in the design workbooks.

Discharge — Manning's Formula IRC SP-13

Manning's Equation for Open Channel Flow

Q = A × (R2/3 × S1/2) / n Where: Q = Design discharge (m³/s = cumecs) A = Cross-sectional area of flow (m²) R = Hydraulic radius = A / P (m) [P = wetted perimeter] S = Bed slope (dimensionless) n = Manning's roughness coefficient Typical n values: Sandy alluvial river (clean): 0.020 – 0.025 Rocky / boulder bed: 0.030 – 0.050 Vegetated banks: 0.025 – 0.035

Lacey's Regime Width

W = 4.75 × Q0.5 (Lacey waterway width, m) Silt factor: f = 1.76 × (m)0.5 [m = median grain size in mm] Sandy alluvial: m ≈ 0.15–0.30 mm → f ≈ 0.85–1.20 Standard f = 1.00 for sandy silt (adopted in most projects)

Molesworth Afflux Formula IRC SP-13 Cl. 7

h* = [ (V² / 17.88) + 0.015 ] × [ (A/a)² − 1 ] Where: h* = Afflux (rise in HFL upstream), m V = Mean velocity in natural channel, m/s A = Natural waterway area (m²) a = Constricted waterway area at bridge (m²) Limit: Afflux h* ≤ 0.30 m for submersible bridges (IRC SP-13 Cl. 7.6)
Scour Depth — IRC:78-1983

Lacey's Normal Scour Depth

dsn = 1.34 × (q² / f)1/3 [Normal scour depth, m below HFL] Where: q = Discharge intensity = Q / L (cumecs/m of waterway width) f = Lacey silt factor (dimensionless) L = Effective waterway width (m) Design scour depths (IRC:78-1983 Cl. 703): Piers (in-stream): Ds = 2.00 × dsn below HFL Abutments (in-bank): Ds = 1.27 × dsn below HFL Foundation depth criterion: Foundation level ≤ HFL − 1.33 × ds (for open foundations)
Submersible Bridge Note: For submersible bridges, the bridge deck level is set BELOW HFL. The afflux therefore acts directly on the structure. Scour is still computed per the full design flood, but the critical check shifts to drag force and buoyancy rather than freeboard.
The BEDACH Load-Accumulation Sequence
WHAT IS BEDACH? BEDACH is the mnemonic for the standard load-accumulation sequence used in IRC pier stability analysis. Each letter represents a force group. The 9 load cases are combinations of these groups applied simultaneously. The sequence builds forces progressively so that the checking engineer can trace every contribution and verify arithmetic at each step. All projects in this suite follow the BEDACH sequence.

Base Pressure Acceptance Criteria

p = V/A ± ML/Zxx ± MT/Zyy Acceptance: pmax ≤ SBC (soil) or allowable rock stress pmin ≥ 0 (no tension at foundation contact) Typical limits in these projects: Soil (sandy alluvial): SBC = 100 – 200 kN/m² Hard rock (top): Allowable = 8,000 kN/m² (8 N/mm²) Tension limit (rock): −3,600 kN/m² (3.6 N/mm² tensile)

Water Current Force Cl. 213.5

P = 52 × K × V² (Force intensity, N/m²) Where: K = Shape coefficient: 0.66 (circular pier), 1.50 (square pier) V = Design velocity at 20° skew = 1.414 × mean velocity Longitudinal component: PL = P × sin²(20°) = 0.117 × P Transverse component: PT = P × cos²(20°) = 0.883 × P

Wind Pressure Cl. 209

Design wind speed Vz = Vb × k₁ × k₂ × k₃ [varies with height, terrain, topography] Wind pressure pz = 0.6 × Vz² (N/m²) Transverse wind on exposed area of deck + pier + parapet Applies in Cases 3, 4, 8, 9 of BEDACH sequence

Impact Factor Cl. 208

I = 4.5 / (6 + L) for IRC Class A/B (L = span in m, I max = 0.50) I = 9 / (13.5 + L) for IRC 70R For L = 7.60 m (SOM Kherwara): I = 4.5/(6+7.60) = 0.331 (33.1%)
IRC:78-1983 — Foundations and Substructure

Scour and Foundation Depth Cl. 703

Minimum founding depth below design scour level: Open foundation on soil: ≥ 1.33 × ds below HFL Open foundation on rock: ≥ 0.60 m embedment in hard rock Well foundation: ≥ 2.00 × ds below HFL (for piers) Typical check in these projects: Foundation level RL < HFL − 1.33 × (Lacey scour depth)

Buoyancy on Substructure Cl. 706

Buoyancy on footing = 100% of displaced water weight (submerged in flood) Buoyancy on pier = 15% reduction factor (per IRC:78-1983 Cl. 706.1) Effective unit weight of pier (with 15% buoyancy): γeff = (24 − 10) × (15/100) correction = 24 × (22.5/24.0) = 22.5 kN/m³ Effective unit weight of footing (100% buoyancy): γeff = 24 − 10 = 14 kN/m³

Abutment Stability Cl. 706

Three failure modes for gravity abutments: 1. Sliding: FOS = (μ × V + passive) / H ≥ 1.50 2. Overturning: FOS = Stabilising moments / Overturning moments ≥ 1.80 3. Bearing: pmax ≤ SBC with adequate pmin ≥ 0 Active earth pressure (Rankine): Ka = (1 − sin φ) / (1 + sin φ) For φ = 30°: Ka = 0.333 For φ = 35°: Ka = 0.271

Slab Design — Effective Width Method IRC SP-13

Effective width beff = α × a × (1 − a/L₀) + b₁ Where: α = Constant depending on b/L₀ ratio (Table 2, IRC SP-13) a = Distance of load from near support L₀ = Effective span of slab b₁ = Width of concentrated load in direction of span

Seismic Compliance IS:1893 + IRC:6 Cl. 222

Seismic Zones (India): II (Low) → III → IV → V (High) Rajasthan projects: Zone II (most projects) — seismic declared non-governing Zone II horizontal coefficient: Ah = (Z/2) × (I/R) × (Sa/g) Z = 0.10 (Zone II), I = 1.5 (bridges), R = 2.5 (confined masonry pier) → Ah = (0.10/2) × (1.5/2.5) × Sa/g = 0.03 × Sa/g Per IRC:6-2016 Cl. 222.1: If Zone II and bridge not on active fault, seismic need only be declared — formal check is advisory only.
