A — Project Scope and Design Basis
RURAL CONNECTIVITY CONTEXT: The designation "T01" identifies this as a template or trunk rural link road in the state rural connectivity programme, analogous to the PMGSY feeder road numbering. The four villages in the route — Jethliya, Sobhaniya, Teendhari, and Chhatri — are small agricultural settlements in the Aravalli foothills of Rajasthan. The unnamed seasonal nala (drainage feature) crossed by this bridge becomes impassable for 2–4 months annually during the monsoon, severing the only motorable link between these villages and the nearest market town. The economic justification for a submersible bridge here is compelling: annual traffic interruption cost (crop spoilage, fuel surcharge, healthcare inaccessibility) substantially exceeds the amortised capital cost of the structure over its 75-year design life. The design follows the IRC SP-13 and IRC:78-1983 methodology appropriate for rural bridges of this category and discharge magnitude.
PROJECT SCOPE: The T01 bridge is a rural link submersible crossing on the Jethliya–Sobhaniya–Teendhari–Chhatri road. Three source workbooks govern the structural design: (i) Stability Analysis XLS — pier stability for all 9 load cases; (ii) Abutment Design XLS — gravity abutment sliding, overturning, and bearing; (iii) Dirt Wall Design XLS — dirt wall section adequacy. Design notes are in DOC format (Git LFS). The design follows the BEDACH load sequence under IRC:6-2016.
HYDRAULIC BASIS (IRC SP-13): For a rural submersible crossing of the T01 designation, the drainage catchment area and the 50-year return-period design flood are established from the hydraulic notes. Manning's equation and the Lacey regime width govern waterway sizing. Molesworth afflux and IRC:78-1983 scour depth fix the foundation level. Buoyancy at 100% applies at design flood; drag forces per IRC:6 Cl. 213 are applied at 20° to the current direction.
B — BEDACH Pier Stability — 9 Load Cases
LOAD ACCUMULATION SEQUENCE: Following the BEDACH convention, loads are accumulated at the pier base in the order: (A) Dead Load (slab, wearing coat, cap, pier, footing) → (B) Live Load (IRC Class-A / 70R maximum reaction) → (C) Water Current and Buoyancy (IRC:6 Cl. 213–214) → (D) Seismic (Zone II, declared non-governing, IRC:6 Cl. 222.1) → (E) Wind (IRC:6 Cl. 212.3) → Base Pressure check (p_max ≤ SBC; p_min ≥ 0).

C — Abutment and Dirt Wall Checks
ABUTMENT (IRC:78-1983): The gravity abutment is checked for the three stability failure modes: sliding (FOS ≥ 1.50), overturning (FOS ≥ 1.80), and bearing (FOS ≥ 2.50). Active earth pressure per Rankine Ka = (1−sinφ)/(1+sinφ). Live-load surcharge over the fill. All results in the Abutment Design XLS (Git LFS). Dirt Wall: The dirt wall between approach slab and abutment cap is checked for shear and bending. Results in Dirt Wall Design XLS (Git LFS).
