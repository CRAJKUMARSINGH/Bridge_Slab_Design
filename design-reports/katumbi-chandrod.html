B — Design Narrative and Acceptance Criteria
HYDRAULIC ANALYSIS: The Katumbi–Chandrod road crosses a seasonal drainage feature. The design discharge is determined from the catchment area using the appropriate return period (typically 50 years for PWD rural submersible bridges). Manning's equation and the Lacey waterway formula govern the hydraulic design. The submersible design accepts overtopping; buoyancy and current forces are the governing structural destabilisers.
STRUCTURAL DESIGN — BEDACH SEQUENCE: (A) Dead Load — slab + cap + pier + footing with 100% buoyancy at design flood. (B) Live Load — IRC Class-A or 70R maximum pier reaction including impact and eccentricity. (C) Water Current and Buoyancy — 52·k·v²·A/100 at 20° per IRC:6 Cl. 213.5; buoyancy per Cl. 214. (D) Seismic — horizontal seismic coefficient per IS:1893 / IRC:6 zone for the project district. (E) Wind — per IRC:6 Cl. 209. Base pressure check: p_max ≤ SBC; p_min ≥ 0 in all nine load cases.
DECK SLAB DESIGN (IRC:112-2015): The slab is designed as a simply supported two-way or one-way slab depending on the span-to-width ratio. Flexural reinforcement in the spanning direction is checked for bending and minimum steel requirements. Distribution steel in the transverse direction follows IRC:21 / IRC:112 guidance. Shear capacity is checked per IRC:112-2015 Cl. 10.3. Crack-width verification per IRC:112-2015 Annexure A ensures long-term durability in the submerged condition.
