D — Water Current and Wind Forces (IRC:6-2016 Cl. 209, 213)
STEPS C and E — WATER CURRENT AND WIND: Per IRC:6-2016 Cl. 213.5, with the bridge at zero degrees skew from the direction of current, design is for 20° in both longitudinal and transverse directions. Design velocity = 1.84 m/s. At 20° skew, longitudinal obstructed velocity = V sin 20° = 0.63 m/s; transverse = V cos 20° = 1.72 m/s (with maximum transverse velocity = 1.414 × mean = 1.414 × 1.84 m/s for the transverse component). Wind pressures are applied per IRC:6 Cl. 212.3, varying by height above bed level. The seismic condition is Zone II and per IRC:6 Cl. 222.1 is not governing; it is recorded explicitly to confirm this was checked.
