A — Project Scope and Source File Register
WHY A HIGH-LEVEL BRIDGE FOR SISARAMA NALAH? The Udaipur–Sisarama road serves a rapidly urbanising periurban corridor on the southern edge of Udaipur city. A submersible bridge on this alignment would be closed for 2–3 months annually during the monsoon — an unacceptable interruption for a road that carries urban bus routes, school buses, emergency vehicles, and growing volumes of commuter traffic. The UIT Udaipur's decision to specify a high-level bridge instead of a submersible design raises the construction cost significantly (deeper abutments, higher deck, approach embankments, retaining walls), but eliminates the traffic closure risk entirely and enables the alignment to function as a year-round urban arterial. The three separate estimate workbooks (bridge, retaining wall, road) reflect this multi-component commitment: the high-level bridge cannot be considered in isolation from its approach road and retaining wall, which together constitute the complete infrastructure package.
PROJECT IDENTITY: The Sisarama Nalah high-level bridge is a UIT Udaipur project on the Udaipur–Sisarama road. It is distinguished from the Sukanaka Nalah and other projects by three unusual features: (i) it is a high-level bridge — requiring an IRC:5-2015 freeboard check — whereas most other projects in the collection are submersible; (ii) it has three separate estimate workbooks (bridge structure, retaining wall, and road) indicating a comprehensive multi-component project; and (iii) the design notes DOC exists in two versions ("Sisarama NALAH" and "Sisarama VILLAGE") suggesting an iterative boundary study. The source files are: Stability XLS, Abutment XLS, Dirt Wall XLS, and three Estimate XLS files, all in Git LFS.

B — Hydraulic Analysis and Freeboard Verification (IRC:5-2015)
HIGH-LEVEL BRIDGE HYDRAULICS (IRC SP-13 + IRC:5-2015): Unlike submersible crossings, the Sisarama Nalah high-level bridge must maintain a minimum clearance between the soffit of the deck and the Design Water Level (HFL + afflux). Per IRC:5-2015 Table 1, the required freeboard is a function of the design discharge tier. The deck soffit level = deck top level minus slab thickness. The freeboard verification: Freeboard = Deck Soffit − (HFL + Afflux). This must equal or exceed the IRC:5-2015 minimum. This check closes the hydraulic section and is a mandatory precondition for issuing construction drawings.
RETAINING WALL CONTEXT: High-level bridges at the ends of the structure often require approach embankments, which in turn require retaining walls where right-of-way is constrained. The "DESINGN OF CANTILEVER RCC RETAINING WALL.xls" and "Estimate Sisarama RETWALL UIT Udaipur.xls" address this component. The "RCC Cantilever Retaining Wall.xls" provides an additional workbook. The retaining wall must be designed for the same earth pressure parameters (φ, γ) used in the abutment design to maintain consistency.
