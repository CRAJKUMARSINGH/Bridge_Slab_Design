Calculation of Reinforcement
-----------------------------

The reinforcement in pile assumed 0.83% of cross-sectional area. 0.83% of c/s area 

Area of Reinforcement = ar_rein = (rpa / 100) * π * (pcdia * 100)^2
                                = (0.830 / 100) * 3.142 * (1.200 * 100)^2
                                = 93.871 Sq.cm 


Bar dia to be provided = bdia = 25 mm 
No. of 25 dia. Bars reqd. = ar_rein / (π * bdia^2 / 400.0) = 19 No. 
                           = 93.871 / (3.14159 * 25.000^2 / 400.0)
                           = 19.123 
                           = 19.000 Nos

            = 22 * (3.14159 * 25.000^2 / 400.0)
